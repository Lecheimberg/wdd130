Full Day Rapids

Spend the entire day on the river tackling exciting rapids while enjoying lunch and breathtaking canyon views. Great for adventurous groups.

Equipment Included

Every trip includes professional rafting guides, helmets, life jackets, paddles, rafts, and safety equipment. Guests should bring comfortable clothing, water shoes, sunscreen, and a refillable water bottle.
